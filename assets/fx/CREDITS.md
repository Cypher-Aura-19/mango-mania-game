Fetched from `cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/jdecked/twemoji@latest/assets/svg/` and vendored
so the game keeps working offline. Used by the take-the-lead celebration in
`assets/celebrate.js`; the confetti itself is drawn in canvas, these are the
poppers and the burst art around it.
